# BeanBox Coffee Co. — Customer FAQ (approved defaults, 31 Oct 2026)

## Products
### What coffee beans do you sell?
Three coffees: Ethiopia Yirgacheffe 250g ($18.00), Colombia Huila 250g ($16.00), and House Espresso 1kg ($42.00).
### What do the coffees taste like?
Ethiopia Yirgacheffe: floral, citrus, tea-like. Colombia Huila: caramel, red apple, cocoa. House Espresso: dark chocolate, low acidity.
### Where do your beans come from?
Ethiopia Yirgacheffe is from Yirgacheffe, Ethiopia. Colombia Huila is from Huila, Colombia.
### What bag sizes are available?
Ethiopia and Colombia come in 250g bags. House Espresso comes in a 1kg bag.
### What merch do you sell?
BeanBox Ceramic Mug ($14.00, 350ml, dishwasher safe), Canvas Tote Bag ($12.00, fits two 1kg bags), Roast Day T-Shirt ($25.00, 100% organic cotton).

## Prices, cart and checkout
### Is tax included in the price?
No. 8% tax is added at checkout, on the subtotal after any discount.
### How do I add items to my cart?
Sign in, then click "Add to cart" on any product. Your cart is on the right side of the shop, and the header shows how many items are in it.
### How do I remove an item or change the quantity?
Clicking "Add to cart" again adds one more. "Remove" next to an item takes one off; the item disappears at zero.
### Do you have discount codes?
When the store offers a code, enter it in the "Coupon code" box in your cart and the discount shows on your total right away.
### How do I check out?
Click "Checkout" under your cart. You'll see an order number (starting with "BB-") and your total, and your cart is emptied. Checkout is disabled when the cart is empty.
### Is this a real store? Will I be charged or receive a delivery?
This is a demo store. Checkout takes no real payment, and there's no real delivery or order tracking.

## Account and feedback
### Do I need an account to shop?
Yes. Sign in to browse beans and merch, use your cart, and report a problem. Click "Log in", or "Create an account" to sign up.
### How do I log out?
Click "Log out" at the top right of the shop.
### How do I report a problem or give feedback?
While signed in, click "Report a problem" at the bottom right of the shop, describe the issue, and click "Send feedback". You can send up to 5 reports per hour.

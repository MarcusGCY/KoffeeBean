# Pelita Health & Beauty: v2 reskin spec for the KoffeeBean demo

**What changed from v1:** the grocery brand (Tidewell Market, still in `/workspace/kb-grocery-reskin/`, untouched) becomes a fictional Singapore **health and beauty / pharmacy** chain. The structure is modelled on a real SG health & beauty e-store; every pixel, word, colour and product here is our own.
**Build target:** a separate demo branch. No prod changes, no real shopper data.
**Mockups:** `00-…07-*.png` plus `overview.png` (contact sheet with legend). Screens are 1440×900, rendered @2x. Source HTML is in `src/html/`, generated by `src/gen.js` with product art from `src/art.js`.
**`ref/`** holds screenshots of the reference site for internal study only. They aren't used in any mockup, and they must **not** go into the repo or the deck.

> Every name, person, order, supplier, ID and number is **fictional demo data**. Every screen shows a dashed **DEMO DATA** tag in the header.

**Visual grammar (unchanged from v1):**
- **Agent:** a violet sparkle and a violet left stripe, labelled "SHELF AGENT · …".

## 3. Type

**Figtree** (Google Font), weights 500 to 800, falling back to `system-ui, sans-serif`. It's friendly and rounded like the reference's look, but a different typeface. Prices and times use `tabular-nums`.

|---|---|---|---|
| `text-display` | 40 / 46 | 800 | Hero headline, big stats |
| `text-h1` | 36 / 44 | 800 | Screen and page titles |
| `text-h2` | 28 / 36 | 800 | Section titles, key numbers, banner headline |
| `text-price` | 30 / 34 | 800 | Card price (36 on the detail page) |
| `text-lg` | 20 / 30 | 600–700 | Product names, field values, buttons |
| `text-base` | 18 / 28 | 500–800 | Body, nav, unit price, stock, ribbons, tags |

Nothing goes below 18px except the "HEALTH & BEAUTY" caps inside the logo and the tiny "SPF50" drawn on an illustration.

## 6. Product card

- **Image well** (128px, `#F8F5F2`, radius 12) with a flat SVG illustration.
  - Top-left **tag**: `New` (rose) or `Bestseller` (ink).
  - Top-right: a **wishlist heart** on a white circle.
  - Bottom edge: a full-width **promo ribbon** (32px, yellow, ink 18/800), e.g. "2 for S$36.00", "2nd at 50% off", "-20%".
- **Name** at 20/700, up to 2 lines · **size** at 18 in `ink-3`.
- **Price** at 30/800. When discounted, the price turns rose and the struck-through was-price sits below it at 18. On the right is a 50px rose **cart button**, grey and disabled when out of stock.
- Optional **member price** chip: "Member S$13.50" (`primary-dark` on `primary-50`).
- **Stock** shows a dot plus text: In stock (`success-text`) · Low stock · 4 left (`warning-text`) · Out of stock (`ink-3`).
- The just-launched product gets a 3px rose border and a soft rose shadow (06).
- The promo patterns are multi-buy, Nth-item % off, % off with was-price, and member price. Use at most **one ribbon per card**, plus an optional member chip.

## 9. The new-product story: Magnesium Glycinate 400 mg, 90 capsules

- **Why it's believable:** magnesium glycinate for sleep and stress is a fast-rising wellness search, and Pelita only stocks magnesium citrate.
- **The gap:** **2,180** searches for "magnesium glycinate" in 14 days, **0** matching products, and **64%** of those searches ending with no purchase. Daily searches climb from 96 to 230 (12–25 Nov, sum exactly 2,180: `[96, 104, 112, 121, 130, 136, 149, 157, 166, 175, 189, 199, 216, 230]`).
- **The proposal:** list it at **S$32.90** (S$0.37/capsule) from **Evergreen Nutrition**, a fictional local supplier. Projected **140 units a week**, **39%** gross margin ex GST (cost S$18.50 against S$30.18 net), confidence **High · 84%**.
- **Approval 1 (sourcing):** **Mei Ling Goh, Category Manager, Vitamins & Supplements**. Up to 600 units (about 4 weeks), spend limit **S$11,500**, recorded as **APR-2051**.
- **Stock check:** the supplier has 6,000 available, a 3-day lead time, S$18.50 cost and an MOQ of 480. **The twist:** the ERP shows **1,200 units already in the Tuas DC**, held for the corporate-wellness (B2B) channel. The agent moves **300 to online today** and drafts **PO-61207 for 600 units at S$11,100**, which is under the limit.
- **Compliance:** the agent's claims check, against fictional demo rules modelled on health-supplement advertising guidance, **removes "cures insomnia"** (a medicinal claim) and keeps "supports relaxation and restful sleep". It passes as **CMP-0412**. This is demo data only and isn't a real HSA determination.
- **Listing and promo:** launch offer **2 for S$55.90**, 25 Nov – 8 Dec. Promo margin is **28%** (S$25.64 net each), above the 15% floor. Projected week-1 sales are **S$3,910** (140 × S$27.95).
- **Approval 2 (go live):** **Arjun Menon, Head of E-commerce**, a different person from approval 1, recorded as **APR-2052**.
- **Live** at 09:09:21. **Total time from detection to live: 7 min 11 s**, with 6 agent steps and 2 human approvals.
- **Pitch line:** *"Shoppers told us what they wanted in the search bar, and the stock was already in our DC. The agent found it, checked the claims, and two people said yes, in about seven minutes."*

## 12. Build notes

- All numbers come from one fixture, `data/demo-scenario.json` (`isDemo: true`): search counts and the daily series, supplier and DC stock, costs, people, IDs and claims-check output.
- Stub the supplier, ERP and claims-check calls with a 600–900 ms delay so the "Checking…" states are visible.
- Approve buttons need the checkbox ticked first. Each approval writes an audit row. Publishing flips `listed: true` and the storefront shows it on the next request, with no redeploy.
- **Projector checks:** 1440×900 at 100% zoom with no scrolling; meaning is never carried by colour alone (every marker has an icon and a word).
- **Out of scope:** payments, real accounts, mobile layouts and real regulatory checks.
